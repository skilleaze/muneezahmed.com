Doing business in Rabat: what I plan around

Rabat is Morocco's capital and an emerging tech hub. The strongest demand for AI, automation and growth work comes from government, technology and education, with many clients based in Hay Riad and Agdal.

Rabat is 3 hours behind Dubai: 10:00 in Rabat is 13:00 in Dubai, and a standard 9:00 to 18:00 day overlaps by about 6 hours, so live calls are easy to schedule.

Customers here mostly reach businesses through WhatsApp. Google is the dominant search engine in Morocco. Paid and organic reach usually comes from Google plus Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

Personal data is governed by Law 09-08, overseen by the CNDP, so every system and campaign is set up to comply from the start.

Work is quoted in MAD or USD, with content and interfaces available in Arabic and French, on a Monday to Friday schedule.
