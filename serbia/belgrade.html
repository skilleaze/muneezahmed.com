Belgrade at a glance

Belgrade's software and gaming companies serve global clients. The strongest demand for AI, automation and growth work comes from IT services, gaming and manufacturing, with many clients based in New Belgrade and Vračar.

Belgrade is 3 hours behind Dubai: 10:00 in Belgrade is 13:00 in Dubai, and a standard 9:00 to 18:00 day overlaps by about 6 hours, so live calls are easy to schedule. The gap moves by an hour when daylight saving time changes.

Customers here mostly reach businesses through Viber and WhatsApp. Google is the dominant search engine in Serbia. Paid and organic reach usually comes from Google plus Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

Personal data is governed by the Law on Personal Data Protection (closely modelled on GDPR), so every system and campaign is set up to comply from the start.

Work is quoted in RSD or USD, with content and interfaces available in Serbian and English, on a Monday to Friday schedule.
