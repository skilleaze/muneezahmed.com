Phuket at a glance

Phuket's resorts and villas compete internationally for travellers. The strongest demand for AI, automation and growth work comes from tourism, hospitality and real estate, with many clients based in Patong and Laguna.

Phuket is 3 hours ahead of Dubai: 10:00 in Phuket is 07:00 in Dubai, and a standard 9:00 to 18:00 day overlaps by about 6 hours, so live calls are easy to schedule.

Customers here mostly reach businesses through LINE. Google is the dominant search engine in Thailand. Paid and organic reach usually comes from Google plus Facebook, LINE, TikTok and YouTube.

Personal data is governed by the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) 2019, so every system and campaign is set up to comply from the start.
