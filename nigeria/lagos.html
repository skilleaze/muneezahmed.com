Doing business in Lagos: what I plan around

Lagos is Africa's leading fintech hub, with mobile-first customers and rapid digital adoption. The strongest demand for AI, automation and growth work comes from fintech, e-commerce and media, with many clients based in Victoria Island and Lekki.

Lagos is 3 hours behind Dubai: 10:00 in Lagos is 13:00 in Dubai, and a standard 9:00 to 18:00 day overlaps by about 6 hours, so live calls are easy to schedule.

Customers here mostly reach businesses through WhatsApp. Google is the dominant search engine in Nigeria. Paid and organic reach usually comes from Google plus Instagram, Facebook, X and TikTok.

Personal data is governed by the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023, so every system and campaign is set up to comply from the start.
