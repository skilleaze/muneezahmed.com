What we understand

THE BUSINESS
  • Self-drive 4x4 rental across Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania and Rwanda
  • A Toyota Land Cruiser fleet: 80 Series, LJ78 Camping, 76 & 78 Series, Prado TX and Station Wagon
  • Customers request a quote for their route, pickup and return point
  • Page to promote: /self-drive-uganda
THE GOAL
  • More quote requests from travellers planning a self-drive safari
  • Run a Search campaign that captures high-intent searches
  • Get the most out of a fixed US$1,500 monthly budget
  • Know exactly which keywords and countries bring bookings

Can US$1,500 a month run an effective campaign? Yes, if it stays focused.

Self-drive 4x4 hire in East Africa is a niche with clear, high-intent searches ("self drive Uganda", "4x4 rental with rooftop tent Kampala"). Instead of spreading the budget over broad travel terms, we put it only on searches from people ready to rent, in the countries they book from, and cut wasted clicks every week. A small budget works best when it is narrow, and that is how this account will be built.

One Google Search campaign, split into tightly themed ad groups so every ad matches the search and every click lands on the self-drive page.
