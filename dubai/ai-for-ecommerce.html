AI for E-commerce in Dubai: what to know

Where AI saves e-commerce brands the most

The biggest savings are usually in customer support, inventory planning and marketing operations. AI agents handle order questions instantly, forecasting reduces stock-outs and overstock, and automation removes manual work between store, warehouse and accounting.

Scaling ads on profit, not ROAS

A high ROAS can still lose money if margins, returns and shipping are ignored. Feeding contribution margin into campaign decisions keeps growth profitable, as seen in results like 10.36x ROAS for Sassind and $200K revenue for Yallatoys.

Selling across the GCC

Expanding from the UAE to Saudi Arabia, Qatar and beyond means new languages, payment methods and shipping rules. AI and automation help manage multiple markets without multiplying headcount.
