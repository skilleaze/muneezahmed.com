The case for an independent AI consultant

Independence matters most when advice affects large decisions. An independent consultant has no software to sell and no sales targets to hit, so recommendations are based purely on what fits your business, budget and team.

How independent consultants handle bigger projects

Strategy, architecture and accountability stay with one senior person. For larger builds, vetted specialists join under that direction, giving you the capacity of a team without losing a single point of responsibility.

Working with an independent AI consultant

Engagements are flexible: a fixed-scope project, a monthly advisory retainer or a fractional AI lead role. Everything is documented and handed over so your business owns the knowledge.
