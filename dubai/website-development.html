Website Development in Dubai: what to know

Choosing the right platform

WordPress suits content-led sites, Shopify suits e-commerce, and frameworks like Next.js suit custom, high-performance sites and web apps. The choice depends on your goals, team and integrations.

Speed, security and Core Web Vitals

Fast, secure websites rank better and convert more. Development includes performance optimisation, SSL, backups and monitoring as standard.

Integrating your website with your business

Forms, bookings, payments and chat should flow directly into your CRM and tools, so enquiries are tracked and followed up automatically.

Building a website that sells in the UAE

A good website is a sales tool first: fast, easy to act on, and measured.

Shopify, WordPress or custom?

The right platform depends on what the site has to do:

PlatformBest forTrade-off
ShopifyOnline stores that need to launch quickly and scaleMonthly platform and app fees
WordPressService businesses, content and SEO-led sitesNeeds good hosting and regular updates
Custom (React / Next.js)Portals, dashboards, bookings and anything bespokeHigher build cost, full flexibility

What every UAE business website needs

These are built in, not added later:

  • A WhatsApp button and click-to-call on every page, tracked as conversions
  • Fast loading on mobile, where most UAE visitors browse
  • Arabic and right-to-left layout support where your customers need it
  • UAE payment options for online sales, such as local card gateways and buy-now-pay-later
  • GA4, conversion tracking and a cookie consent setup that respects the UAE Personal Data Protection Law
  • Technical SEO from day one: clean URLs, schema markup, sitemaps and fast Core Web Vitals
