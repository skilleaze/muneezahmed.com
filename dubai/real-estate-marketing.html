How real estate marketing works in Dubai

Off-plan launches vs ready and secondary listings

Off-plan campaigns sell a project and a payment plan, usually to investors in the UAE and abroad, so they lean on Meta, Snapchat, TikTok and YouTube video with lead forms and a fast WhatsApp follow-up. Ready and secondary listings sell a specific home to people already searching, so Google Search and the property portals do most of the work, supported by remarketing.

Lead quality beats lead volume

Cheap leads are easy; buyers are not. Campaigns ask the questions that matter to an agent: budget, timeline, cash or mortgage, and whether the buyer is an investor or an end user. Scoring and routing happen automatically, so the best leads reach an agent on WhatsApp within minutes, and the rest go into a nurture sequence instead of being forgotten.

Rules every Dubai property ad must follow

Property ads in Dubai need a Trakheesi advertising permit from the Dubai Land Department before they go live, and off-plan projects may only be marketed once they are registered with the escrow account department. Brokers need a valid RERA licence, and individuals promoting property on their own social accounts need a UAE Advertiser Permit. Customer data used in retargeting falls under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law. Compliance is checked before launch, so campaigns are not paused halfway through a sales window.
