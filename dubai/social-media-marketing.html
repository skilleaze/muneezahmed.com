Social Media Marketing in Dubai: what to know

Choosing the right platforms in the UAE

Instagram and TikTok lead for consumer brands, LinkedIn for B2B and Snapchat for younger audiences. Focusing on the platforms your buyers actually use beats trying to be everywhere.

From followers to customers

Growth in followers only matters if it leads to enquiries and sales. Clear calls to action, WhatsApp links, retargeting and lead capture connect social activity to revenue.

Consistency and community

Regular, on-brand posting and fast replies to comments and messages build trust. A content calendar and clear response process keep this consistent.
