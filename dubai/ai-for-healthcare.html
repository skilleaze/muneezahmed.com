AI for Clinics & Healthcare in Dubai: what to know

Reducing no-shows and admin in clinics

No-shows and phone admin are two of the biggest hidden costs for clinics. Automated confirmations, reminders and easy rescheduling on WhatsApp cut no-shows, while an AI booking agent handles routine calls and messages so the front desk can focus on patients in the clinic.

Patient journeys, not just appointments

A clinic CRM tracks each patient from first enquiry to treatment and follow-up, making recalls, reviews and repeat visits systematic. This is how Dubai Smile Dental grew appointments by 300% and Lana Life Care generated $180K revenue.

Compliance in healthcare marketing

Healthcare advertising in the UAE has specific rules. Campaigns and content are planned with these requirements in mind, and patient data is handled with strict access controls.
