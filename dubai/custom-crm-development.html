Custom CRM Development in Dubai: what to know

Custom CRM vs HubSpot, Zoho or Salesforce

Off-the-shelf CRMs are a good start, but many Dubai businesses outgrow them. Per-user fees rise as the team grows, WhatsApp is bolted on rather than built in, and the pipeline never quite matches how you sell. A custom CRM fits your process exactly, has no per-user licences and connects directly to your ads, WhatsApp, accounting and ERP.

What a custom CRM should include

At minimum: a pipeline that mirrors your real sales stages, automatic lead capture from forms, ads, portals and WhatsApp, follow-up reminders and task automation, role-based dashboards and clean reporting. AI adds lead scoring, next-best-action suggestions and forecasts, so your team knows who to call today and management can see where revenue is heading.

How long and how much

A focused first version is typically ready in 6 to 10 weeks, then extended in phases. Pricing is one quotation for the build, fully white-labelled in your brand, plus a small yearly fee for hosting, support and updates. Over three to five years this is often cheaper than paying per-user subscriptions for a tool that only partly fits.
