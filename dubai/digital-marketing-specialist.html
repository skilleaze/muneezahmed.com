Digital Marketing Specialist in Dubai: what to know

What a digital marketing specialist does differently

A specialist owns results across channels instead of managing one platform in isolation. That means deciding where budget should go, building and optimising the campaigns, fixing tracking and improving the website, then reporting on revenue rather than vanity metrics.

How to choose a digital marketing specialist in Dubai

Ask for case studies with real numbers, check they can explain tracking and attribution clearly, and confirm who will actually manage your account. A good specialist should challenge your assumptions, show where money is being wasted and set targets they are willing to be measured against.
