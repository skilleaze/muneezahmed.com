Digital Marketing Specialist in Dubai: what to know

What a digital marketing specialist does differently

A specialist owns results across channels instead of managing one platform in isolation. That means deciding where budget should go, building and optimising the campaigns, fixing tracking and improving the website, then reporting on revenue rather than vanity metrics.

How to choose a digital marketing specialist in Dubai

Ask for case studies with real numbers, check they can explain tracking and attribution clearly, and confirm who will actually manage your account. A good specialist should challenge your assumptions, show where money is being wasted and set targets they are willing to be measured against.

Results you can expect

Every market is different, but client campaigns have averaged 6.2x ROAS, with examples such as 10.36x for Sassind Melbourne and 12x for Kaye & Co Real Estate in Dubai. The first weeks focus on tracking and quick wins; scaling follows once the data proves what works.

What a digital marketing specialist in Dubai does

Hands-on execution across the channels that bring UAE customers, measured on leads and sales rather than clicks.

Specialist, consultant or agency?

A digital marketing specialist builds and runs the campaigns: ad accounts, keywords, audiences, creative tests, landing pages and tracking. A digital marketing consultant sets strategy and oversees others. An agency adds a team for volume. If your problem is that campaigns are not performing, you need a specialist who works inside the accounts every week.

What a digital marketing specialist costs in Dubai

You pay two things: the ad budget, which goes directly to Google, Meta, Snapchat or TikTok in AED, and a fee for managing the campaigns. Fees depend on the number of channels and campaigns. You get one proposal after a free call, with scope, timeline and total agreed before work starts.

How to hire the right specialist

Check these five things before you hire:

  • Case studies with real numbers, ideally in your industry and the UAE
  • Ad accounts, pixels and analytics set up in your company's name
  • Tracking fixed first, so every lead from WhatsApp, calls and forms is counted
  • Weekly or monthly reports on leads, sales and cost per acquisition
  • Knowledge of UAE rules: Advertiser Permits for creators, DHA rules for health ads and Trakheesi permits for property ads
