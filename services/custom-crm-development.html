Custom CRM Development: what to know

Why companies build a custom CRM

Teams build their own CRM when standard tools no longer fit their sales process, when per-user licence costs keep rising, or when they need deep integration with messaging, operations and finance systems.

AI inside your CRM

AI turns a CRM from a database into an assistant: scoring leads, suggesting the next best action, summarising conversations and forecasting revenue so teams focus on the right deals.

Owning your CRM

A custom CRM is white-labelled in your brand, and you own the data and the system. A small yearly fee covers hosting, support and improvements.

Frequently asked questions

Why build a custom CRM instead of using HubSpot or Salesforce?

When your process is specific, a custom CRM fits better, avoids per-user fees and connects exactly to your tools.

How long does a custom CRM take?

A focused first version is typically ready in 6 to 10 weeks, then extended in phases.

Can you migrate our existing data?

Yes. Contacts, deals and history are migrated from spreadsheets or your current CRM.

How is it priced?

One quotation for the build, then a small yearly fee for hosting, support and updates.
