Doing business in Hong Kong: what I plan around

Hong Kong businesses operate at high speed across Asian markets, making automation and multilingual AI valuable. The strongest demand for AI, automation and growth work comes from financial services, trade and retail, with many clients based in Central and Kowloon.

Hong Kong is 4 hours ahead of Dubai: 10:00 in Hong Kong is 06:00 in Dubai, and a standard 9:00 to 18:00 day overlaps by about 5 hours, so live calls are easy to schedule.

Customers here mostly reach businesses through WhatsApp and WeChat. Google is the dominant search engine in Hong Kong. Paid and organic reach usually comes from Google plus Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and Xiaohongshu (RED).

Personal data is governed by the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (PDPO), so every system and campaign is set up to comply from the start.

Work is quoted in HKD or USD, with content and interfaces available in Cantonese, English and Mandarin, on a Monday to Friday schedule.
