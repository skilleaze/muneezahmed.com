Working with Beijing companies

Beijing is a leading centre for AI research and large technology firms. The strongest demand for AI, automation and growth work comes from technology, government and education, with many clients based in Chaoyang and Zhongguancun.

Beijing is 4 hours ahead of Dubai: 10:00 in Beijing is 06:00 in Dubai, and a standard 9:00 to 18:00 day overlaps by about 5 hours, so live calls are easy to schedule.

Customers here mostly reach businesses through WeChat. Baidu is the leading search engine in mainland China, and Google Search is not accessible there without a VPN in China. Paid and organic reach usually comes from Google plus WeChat, Douyin, Xiaohongshu (RED) and Weibo.

Personal data is governed by the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL), so every system and campaign is set up to comply from the start.

Work is quoted in CNY or USD, with content and interfaces available in Mandarin Chinese and English, on a Monday to Friday schedule.
